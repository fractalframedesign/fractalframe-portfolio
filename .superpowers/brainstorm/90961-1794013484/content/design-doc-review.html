Why

Today the home page reads like a marketing page. The new one should feel like you talking, calm and personal, and it should show work you've actually made.

The five parts

  • 1 · Intro: your time pill, your photo with your name and role, then your three paragraphs, word for word.
  • 2 · What I do: four tiles, each with a small drawing, a title and one sentence. Your About page gets these too.
  • 3 · Highlights: your three showcase projects with their cover images, a bit wider than the text. Each opens in a new tab.
  • 4 · Writing: your same five recent articles as a quiet list, plus "Read all".
  • 5 · Work: your four jobs as simple rows, with dates on the right.

Leaving the home page

  • Principles: already on your About page, so it was showing twice.
  • Design to motivate + the pyramid: it's already a full article, so it becomes the first row in Writing.
  • The big headline, the buttons, the tags and icons, and the skills list in Experience.

Calls I made (please check)

  • Your role sits under your name in place of "Tokyo", since the pill already says Tokyo.
  • "Hi, I'm Kiran" is gone, since your name row already says who you are.
  • Highlights: cover images, no year, opens in a new tab.
  • Caveat for the handwritten titles.

Not part of this

The About page (we'll design it next), the menu, the footer, and the Projects and Articles pages.

What counts as failing

Wording or a look so close to the reference that it reads as someone else's site, clutter creeping back, or text that sounds machine-written.
