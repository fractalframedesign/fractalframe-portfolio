Storyboard v2: reviewed and tightened

Same idea, "one anatomy, many widgets", with fewer beats, longer holds, one continuous camera and an ending that lands on the product.

What was wrong with v1

  • Too fast to read. Five widgets in five seconds: nobody can take in a number in under ~1.2s.
  • No holds. Every beat moved. Great motion is mostly stillness with a few confident moves.
  • Hard cut feeling at the pull back. The hero widget didn't have a fixed home in the wall.
  • Weak ending. Fading to empty outlines ends on nothing; it should end on the product.
  • No colour story. All grey; nothing guides the eye.

Rules for v2

  • One camera, no cuts. Every shot grows out of the last.
  • Three morphs, 1.4s each: 0.5s move, 0.9s hold.
  • One motion language: springs with no bounce for parts, a slow ease for the camera, 40ms stagger.
  • One accent colour: blue only on the labels and the hero tile; everything else neutral.
  • Numbers roll, never fade. Fixed-width digits, so nothing jiggles.
