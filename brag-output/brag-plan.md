# Brag plan: Rakuten Link (portfolio cover video)

**What it is:** Rakuten Link, the free calling and messaging app for Rakuten Mobile customers, on phone and desktop. Kiran designed it from the first idea to launch.
**Who it's for:** hiring managers and design leads visiting Kiran's portfolio (the video is the project cover, plays muted on loop).
**What sets it apart:** unlimited domestic calls and free messages, one design system across the phone and desktop apps.
**Hook:** "Call anyone in Japan." then "Free." on Link pink.
**Visual identity:** Rakuten Mobile foundation colours (pink #FF008C, ground blue #000033, UI blue #3B3BA3), Rubik for display, Noto Sans for text. Real components from the portfolio's Link demo (LinkMobileApp, CallScreen, ChatThread, LinkDesktopApp).
**Data:** only the demo's made-up contacts (Aiko Tanaka, Farah Khan…) and numbers. No real names, numbers or account details.
**Tone:** polished, punchy (default leaning polished).
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt (portfolio not launched: do not post).

## Music
120 BPM, 4/4, F major (Fmaj7 · Am7 · Dm7 · Bbmaj7), 1 bar = 2 s = 60 frames at 30 fps. Every cut lands on a beat (15 frames).
